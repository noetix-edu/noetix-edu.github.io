FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need coding experience?+
No prior coding or technical experience is required. The course starts from absolute fundamentals and progressively builds your understanding through guided, hands-on lessons. You will write some simple code by the end, but we teach you everything from scratch.
How long does the course take?+
The course runs for 12 weeks with 2–3 sessions per week, each lasting approximately 45–60 minutes. The final module includes a capstone project which students present to a panel. Total commitment is around 6–8 hours per week.
Do I get a certificate?+
Yes. Every student who completes the course and final project earns a Noetix AI Foundations Certificate. The certificate is verifiable online and recognised by our partner schools. It is designed to strengthen university and scholarship applications.
Is the course beginner friendly?+
Absolutely. This course was designed specifically for students with no technical background. The curriculum is structured to be challenging yet accessible — every complex concept is broken down with real-world analogies, visual explanations, and hands-on exercises.
How are the sessions delivered?+
Sessions are delivered in small cohorts of up to 20 students — either on-campus through our school partnerships or in a structured online format. Each session is live and interactive, not pre-recorded. Students get real-time feedback from the instructor.
What grade levels is this for?+
The AI Foundations course is designed for students in Grades 8–12 (ages 13–18). The content is calibrated for this age group — intellectually rigorous but approachable, with projects scoped to be achievable in the given timeframe.
