FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What age group is Noetix designed for?+
Noetix programs are designed for students in Grades 8–12, aged 12–18. Accessible regardless of prior technical knowledge.
Do students need prior coding experience?+
No prior experience is required. Each track starts with foundational concepts and progressively builds to more advanced topics through guided, project-based lessons.
Are Noetix certificates recognized?+
Noetix certificates are industry-aligned and recognized by our partner schools, reflecting competencies in AI, cybersecurity, and programming.
How are classes conducted?+
In cohorts of up to 20 students for focused engagement. Programs are delivered on-campus in partnership with schools, or in a hybrid format.
How long does each program take?+
Most programs run 10–12 weeks, structured to fit alongside school schedules — typically 2–3 sessions per week with a final capstone project.
How can my school partner with Noetix?+
Email hello@noetix.edu or use the Partner button in the Schools section. We'll schedule an intro call and propose a pilot cohort plan tailored to your school.
